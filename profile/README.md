### 🌐 About Gayatri Education

**Gayatri Education**, operated under **DBERT Technical Incubation** (MSME Reg: `UDYAM-UP-29-0109046`), engineers state-of-the-art educational infrastructure, enterprise management platforms, and autonomous intelligence systems. 

Our core research focuses on **air-gapped local RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**, **course-independent Socratic tutoring**, and **full-stack multi-tenant ERP platforms** that empower academic institutions and commercial training centers.
